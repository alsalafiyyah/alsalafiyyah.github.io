### Question: 

Is the phrase "Lord of the Qur'an" correct to use?

### Answer: 

Do not say this, because the Jahmites (Jahmiyyah) say that the Qur'an is created. If you say "Lord of the Qur'an," you imply that you are a Jahmite. Would you accept for yourself that people think you are a Jahmite?
