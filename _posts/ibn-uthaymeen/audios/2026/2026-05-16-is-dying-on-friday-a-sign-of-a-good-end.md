### Question: 

In the last question from this inquirer: Is dying on Friday a sign of a good end?

### Answer: 

No, death happens equally on any day. If any day had a special privilege, Monday would have been more deserving of it, because it is the day on which the Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him and his family) passed away. However, I do not know of any specific day that holds a special privilege regarding death. Yes
