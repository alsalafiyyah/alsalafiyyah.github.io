### Question: 

If we judge a Jew or a Christian to be a kafir (disbeliever), does it necessarily follow that they will enter Hellfire?

### Answer: 

Yes, there is no doubt. The Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) said: **"There is no one from my nation who hears of what I have been sent with, and then does not believe in it, but that he will be of the companions of the Fire,"** and he explicitly mentioned Jews and Christians **"By the One in Whose hand is my soul, no one from this nation hears of me—neither a Jew nor a Christian—and then does not believe in what I have been sent with, but that he will be of the companions of the Fire."**
