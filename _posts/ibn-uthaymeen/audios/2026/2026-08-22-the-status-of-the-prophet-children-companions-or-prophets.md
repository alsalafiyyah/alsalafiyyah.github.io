### Question: 

Were the children of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ considered Sahabah (Companions) or Prophets? May Allah reward you with goodness.

### ​Answer: 
The children of the Prophet ﷺ are considered Sahabah (Companions); this is because Allah says: **"Muhammad (ﷺ) is not the father of any of your men, but he is the Messenger of Allâh and the last (end) of the Prophets."** [Al-Ahzab: 40] 

If his children had been prophets, they would have come after him.
