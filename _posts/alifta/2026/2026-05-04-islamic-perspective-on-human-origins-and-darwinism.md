### Question: 

What is the truth regarding the origin of human beings? Is it true that the first humans who lived millions of years ago had tails, and what is the opinion regarding what some Western scientists claim about human origins?

### ​Answer: 

The claim that the origin of man is a monkey is a lie and a fabrication against the law of Allah. Rather, the father of mankind and original source is Adam, peace and blessings be upon him, and Adam was created from clay.
