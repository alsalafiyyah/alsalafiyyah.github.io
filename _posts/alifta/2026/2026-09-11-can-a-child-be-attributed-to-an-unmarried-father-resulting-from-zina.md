### Question:

What is the ruling regarding a child of zina if the father confesses [to paternity], but the mother is not married?

### Answer:

The lineage of the child is not established for the adulterer through zina, based on the saying of the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him: **'The child belongs to (the owner of) the bed [the legal husband], and for the adulterer there is stoning.'** 

It is permissible for the adulterer to marry the adulteress after the expiration of the waiting period (iddah) and sincere repentance. 

And success is from Allah, and may Allah send blessings and peace upon our Prophet Muhammad, his family, and his companions.
