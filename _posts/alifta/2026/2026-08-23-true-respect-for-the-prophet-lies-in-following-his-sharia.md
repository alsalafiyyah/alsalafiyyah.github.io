### Question: 

What is the ruling on celebrating the birthday of the Prophet (peace be upon him) in the month of Rabi' al-Awwal out of reverence for him, peace and blessings be upon him?

### Answer: 

Revering and respecting the Prophet is done solely by: believing in everything he brought from Allah, following his Shariah in creed, speech, action, and character, and abstaining from introducing innovations (bid'ah) into the religion. Celebrating the birthday of the Prophet (peace be upon him) is considered an innovation in the religion.

And with Allah is all success. May Allah send peace and blessings upon our Prophet Muhammad, his family, and his companions.
