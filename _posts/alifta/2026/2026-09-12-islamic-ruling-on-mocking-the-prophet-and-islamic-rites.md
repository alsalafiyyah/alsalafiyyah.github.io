### Question: 

What is the ruling on mocking anything from the religion of the Messenger, may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him?

### Answer: 

Mocking anything from the religion of the Messenger, may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him, is apostasy from the religion of Islam. Allah Almighty has said: **"Say: 'Was it at Allah, and His Ayat (proofs, evidences, verses, lessons, signs, revelations, etc.) and His Messenger (ﷺ) that you were mocking?' Make no excuse; you have disbelieved after you had believed."** [At-Tawbah, 65-66]

​And success comes from Allah, and may Allah send peace and blessings upon our Prophet Muhammad, his family, and his companions.
