### Question: 

What are the origins and principles of the Salafi Da'wah?

### Answer:

The Salafi Da'wah is: the call to that which the Messenger ﷺ, his companions (may Allah be pleased with them), and their followers in righteousness were upon, through wisdom, good instruction, and arguing in a way that is best, alongside striving against oneself to act upon what one calls to.
