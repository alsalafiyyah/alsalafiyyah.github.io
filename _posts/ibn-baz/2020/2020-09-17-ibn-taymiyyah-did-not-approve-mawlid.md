### Question: 

The brother A. M. M. from Kuwait says in his question: "One of the scholars mentioned that Imam Ibn Taymiyyah (may Allah have mercy on him) viewed the celebration of the Prophet's Birthday (Mawlid) as something commendable. Is this true, O Your Eminence the Sheikh?"

### Answer: 

Celebrating the Prophet's Birthday (Mawlid)—upon its owner be the best of prayers and purest of salutations—is an innovation (bid'ah) that is not permissible according to the most correct opinion of the scholars. **This is because the Prophet ﷺ did not do it, nor did his Rightly-Guided Caliphs, nor all of his Companions (may Allah be pleased with them), nor the scholars and rulers during the three preferred centuries.** Rather, it occurred after that time because of the Shiites and those who imitated them, so it is permissible neither to practice it nor to imitate those who do.

And Sheikh Taqi al-Din Ahmad ibn Taymiyyah (may Allah have mercy on him) is among those who reject this and view it as bid'ah (an innovation). However, in his book "Iqtida' al-Sirat al-Mustaqim Mukhalafat Ashhab al-Jahim," he mentioned regarding those who do it out of ignorance. 

It is not fitting for anyone to be deceived by those people who practice it, endorse it, or call to it—such as Muhammad Alawi al-Maliki and others—because the proof does not lie in the statements of men; rather, the proof lies in what Allah the Glorified said, what His Messenger ﷺ said, or what the predecessors of the Ummah agreed upon. 

This is based on the saying of Allah the Exalted: **“O you who believe! Obey Allâh and obey the Messenger (Muhammad ﷺ), and those of you (Muslims) who are in authority. (And) if you differ in anything amongst yourselves, refer it to Allâh and His Messenger (صلى الله عليه وسلم), if you believe in Allâh and in the Last Day. That is better and more suitable for final determination.”** [An-Nisa': 59]; 

His saying the Glorified: **"“And in whatsoever you differ, the decision thereof is with Allâh (He is the ruling Judge).”** [Ash-Shura: 10]; 

And His saying the Glorified: **“Indeed in the Messenger of Allâh (Muhammad ﷺ) you have a good example to follow for him who hopes for (the Meeting with) Allâh and the Last Day, and remembers Allâh much.”** [Al-Ahzab: 21].

And he (upon him be peace and blessings) did not do that, and he conveyed the clear message through his words and actions ﷺ, and his Companions (may Allah be pleased with them) did not do it either. Had it been a good thing, they would have preceded us to it.

The Prophet ﷺ said: **"Whoever introduces into this matter of ours (Islam) something that is not part of it will have it rejected"** [Agreed upon by Bukhari and Muslim] [^1].

He (upon him be peace and blessings) also said: **"Whoever performs an action that is not in accordance with our matter [Islam], it will be rejected"** (Reported by Muslim in his Sahih) [^2].

He ﷺ used to say in his sermons: **"To proceed: The best speech is the Book of Allah, the best guidance is the guidance of Muhammad ﷺ, the worst of matters are those newly invented, and every bid'ah (innovation) is a misguidance"** (Reported by Muslim in his Sahih) [^3].

And the Hadiths bearing this meaning are numerous. I have written at some length regarding this matter, as well as concerning other innovations—such as the celebrations of the Night of Al-Isra' wal-Mi'raj (Night Journey and Ascension) and the middle of Sha'ban.

All of these have been published in a booklet entitled Al-Tahdhir min al-Bida' (Warning Against Innovations), which is distributed by the General Presidency of Scholarly Research and Ifta and the Ministry of Islamic Affairs. It is also available in my book entitled Majmoo' Fatawa wa Maqalat (Collection of Fatwas and Articles), volume 1, page 227. Whoever wishes to review it may do so.

We ask Allah to grant success to us and to all Muslims in knowing the truth and following it, and to protect us all from innovations and evil deeds, both apparent and hidden. Indeed, He is the Guardian of that and is capable of it. May Allah send peace and blessings upon our Prophet Muhammad, his family, and his Companions.

[^1]: Al-Bukhari, Sahih, Book on reconciliation, no. 2697; Muslim, Sahih, Book on judicial decisions, no. 1718; Abu Dawud, Sunan, Book on Al-Sunnah, no. 4606; Ibn Majah, Sunan, Introduction, no. 14; and Ahmad, Musnad, vol. 6, p. 240.
[^2]: Muslim, Sahih, Book on judicial decisions, no. 1718; and Ahmad ibn Hanbal, Musnad, vol. 6, p. 256.
[^3]: Muslim, Sahih, Book on Friday, no. 867; Al-Nasa'i, Sunan, Book on the two 'Eid Prayers, no. 1578; Abu Dawud, Book on tribute, spoils, and rulership, no. 2954; Ibn Majah, Sunan, Introduction, no. 45; Ahmad ibn Hanbal, Musnad, vol. 3, p. 311; and Al-Darimy, Sunan, Introduction, no. 206.
