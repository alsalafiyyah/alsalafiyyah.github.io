### Question:

[What is meant by] the saying of the Prophet ﷺ: **"I am free (disassociated or disavow) from anyone [Muslim] who resides among the mushrikeen (polytheists)"**?
​
### Answer:

[His ﷺ saying] **"...disassociates from every Muslim who resides among the polytheists"** applies if they are capable [of leaving]. 

However, if they are unable, they should fear Allah; once they become capable, they should migrate. If they are unable, they are excused, but they must still fear Allah.
