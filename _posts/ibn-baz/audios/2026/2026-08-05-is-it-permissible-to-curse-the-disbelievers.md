### Question: 

Regarding the statement of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, "May the curse of Allah be upon the Jews," is it permissible to curse the Jews?

### Answer: 

Yes, curse them. May Allah curse the Jews, may Allah curse the Christians, and may Allah curse the disbelievers, just as the Prophet ﷺ cursed them.
