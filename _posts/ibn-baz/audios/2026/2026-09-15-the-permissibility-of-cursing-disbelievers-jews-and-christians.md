### Question: 

Regarding the saying of the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him: **'May the curse of Allah be upon the Jews,'** is it permissible to curse the Jews?

### Answer: 

Yes, curse them. May Allah curse the Jews, may Allah curse the Christians, and may Allah curse the disbelievers, just as the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, cursed them.
