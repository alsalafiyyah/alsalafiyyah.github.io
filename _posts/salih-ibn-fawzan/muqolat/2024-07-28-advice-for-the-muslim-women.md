### First Sermon 

Praise be to Allah, Who created the male and the female, and made a distinction between them: "And the male is not like the female."

​I bear witness that there is no deity worthy of worship except Allah alone, without any partner; to Him belong the most beautiful names. And I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and Messenger, whom his Lord addressed by saying: **"And your Lord is going to give you, and you will be satisfied."**

​May the peace and blessings of Allah be upon him, his family, his companions, and those who are guided by his guidance, adhere to his Sunnah, and act upon his counsel; and may He grant them abundant peace.

​To proceed:

​O people! Fear Allah the Almighty, and know that Allah (Glorified and Exalted be He) has placed upon you the responsibility of women, by granting you guardianship over them. Allah Almighty says: **"Men are the caretakers of women, as Allah has given some more than others and because they support them from their means."**

​The Prophet ﷺ also placed the responsibility of women upon you, saying in his farewell sermon during the Farewell Pilgrimage: **"Treat women well."** And he warned against their temptation by saying (may the peace and blessings of Allah be upon him): **"Beware of the world and beware of women, for the first trial of the Children of Israel was through women."**

And he ﷺ said: **"I have not left after me any temptation more harmful to men than women."**

​This is because women are complementary in nature regarding intellect and religious practice, as stated in the authentic Hadith. Therefore, guardianship over them is absolutely essential. It is vital to exercise guardianship over them by safeguarding their religion, their morals, and their modest conduct, even before providing for their financial upkeep, clothing, and other material needs. Whether they are wives, daughters, sisters, or other female relatives under your care—you are responsible for them.

​Furthermore, they have been strictly warned with the severest of consequences if they fail to preserve their femininity, their morals, and their dignity. Allah Almighty says: **"And tell the believing women to reduce [some] of their vision and guard their private parts and not expose their adornment... except to their husbands..."** [to the end of the verse].

​And He (Glorified and Exalted be He) says: **"O Prophet, tell your wives and your daughters and the women of the believers to draw their outer garments close over themselves. That is more suitable that they will be known and not be abused."**

Women are a tremendous responsibility upon men. If a woman is left to her own devices, she may stray from the correct path, enticed by temptations, advocates of misguidance, bad role models, and proponents of Westernization, who divert her from the natural path that Allah set for her. Therefore, fear Allah regarding your women, and protect them.

​O brothers! It is indeed deeply shameful and heartbreaking to see the loss of so many women these days, doing whatever they wish and desire of blameworthy morals and disastrous traits. They neither feel shame nor fear Allah (Glorified and Exalted be He). Moreover, they have no men to guide or exercise authority over them; rather, they are neglected and lost, except for those upon whom Allah has mercy.

​O woman! The Prophet ﷺ informed us that the majority of the inhabitants of Hellfire are women. Why? Because they deny favor and show ingratitude to their husbands. Thus, they comprise the majority of the people of Hellfire.

​The Prophet ﷺ said: **"There are two types of the people of Hellfire whom I have not yet seen: men with whips like the tails of oxen, with which they beat the people"**—and these are the tyrannical rulers and oppressive police who beat people unlawfully; this is the first type. 

And the second type are **women who are clothed yet naked, walking with an enticing gait and leading others astray, with their heads looking like the humps of camels leaning to one side. They will not enter Paradise, nor will they even smell its fragrance, even though its fragrance can be detected from such-and-such a distance"**—and in some narrations, **"from a distance of five hundred years."** They will not enter Paradise, nor will they smell its fragrance.

This is a severe warning to women, and to their guardians whom Allah has entrusted with their responsibility.

**​"Clothed yet naked"** means that they cover parts of their bodies while exposing the rest and exposing the areas of temptation. This is exactly what is happening now right in your midst—in wedding halls, in lounges, and in women's gatherings. They come clothed yet naked, wearing a few pieces of fabric while the rest of their bodies are bare; exposing their backs, their chests, their upper arms, their forearms, and their legs. They arrive like this while other women look at them. This is why the Prophet said, **"clothed yet naked."** They wear some clothing on parts of their bodies, while the enticing parts of the body are left exposed—we seek refuge with Allah.

​This happens in parties; it has increased and diversified in social gatherings, with no monitor and no accountability.

**"Deviating [from the truth]"**: they deviate from the truth, and it is also said that they sway in their walk, strutting and swaying to cause temptation. **"Leading others astray"**: acting as a bad example for those who see them, thereby bearing their own sins as well as the sins of others. They are deviating and leading others astray; when they gather in these venues, other women and young girls who look at them follow their example.

Thus, they turn other women away from the truth, from concealment, and from modesty.

**"Their heads looking like the humps of camels"** means that they artificially enlarge their heads with hairpieces and padding so that it looks as though they have two heads: their natural head, and an artificial head created by the unsightly things they wrap onto it. If you look at such a woman walking anywhere, you find her head unnaturally enlarged. The Messenger of Allah ﷺ spoke the absolute truth: **"their heads looking like the humps of camels leaning to one side. They will not enter Paradise."**

This is a severe warning; we ask Allah for safety. 

Therefore, it is incumbent upon the woman and her guardian to repent to Allah (Glorified and Exalted be He) before death arrives and before it is too late. Do not take matters concerning women lightly. Her guardian is her husband; her guardian is her father; her guardian is her son; her guardian is her brother; her guardian is her male relatives. They must keep the woman's conduct in check, for if a woman is left to her own whims, she will become lost.

​A woman is complementary in her intellect and religious duties, lacking the full capacity to manage independently in these matters; she is in constant need of someone to guide and care for her. She is like a reckless person or one lacking judgment who, if left entirely to their own devices, would destroy themselves, waste their wealth, and ruin their religion.

​So fear Allah, O servants of Allah, regarding your women, your daughters, your wives, and all the women of the Muslims.

And know that Allah (Glorified and Exalted be He) has mentioned that among men there are believers, hypocrites, and polytheists, just as He mentioned that among women there are believers, polytheists, and hypocrites.

​Allah Almighty says: **"The hypocrite men and hypocrite women are of one another. They enjoin what is wrong and forbid what is right and close their hands. They have forgotten Allah, so He has forgotten them [accordingly]. Indeed, the hypocrites—it is they who are the defiantly disobedient. Allah has promised the hypocrite men and hypocrite women and the disbelievers the fire of Hell, wherein they will abide eternally. It is sufficient for them. And Allah has cursed them, and for them is an enduring punishment."** until He, the Almighty, says: **"The believing men and believing women are allies of one another. They enjoin what is right and forbid what is wrong and establish prayer and give Zakat and obey Allah and His Messenger. Those—Allah will have mercy upon them. Indeed, Allah is Exalted in Might and Wise. Allah has promised the believing men and believing women gardens beneath which rivers flow, wherein they abide eternally, and pleasant dwellings in gardens of perpetual residence; but approval from Allah is greater. It is that which is the great attainment."**

So fear Allah, O servants of Allah, and examine yourselves and your women to see which of the two groups you belong to. Fear Allah within yourselves! The Quran is recited to you day and night, and you hear the Sunnah day and night, regarding yourselves and your women. So where is the action upon the Book and the Sunnah? Shall every negligent person do whatever they wish? Every irresponsible person? There is no might nor power except with Allah; they are like cattle, nay, they are even further astray from the path.

​So fear Allah, O servants of Allah! 

**"Protect yourselves and your families from a Fire."** 

**"O you who have believed, protect yourselves and your families from a Fire whose fuel is people and stones, over which are [appointed] angels, harsh and severe; they do not disobey Allah in what He commands them but do what they are commanded."***

​Restrain your women [from wrongdoing], and pay close attention to them—especially during parties and social gatherings.

The Prophet ﷺ said: **"Do not prevent the female servants of Allah from attending the mosques of Allah, though their houses are better for them. And let them go out unperfumed"**—meaning without adornments, without perfume, and without displaying their beauty. The Mother of the Believers, Aisha (may Allah be pleased with her), said: **"If the Messenger of Allah (may the peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) could see what women have introduced [of innovations and adornments], he would have forbidden them from attending the mosques, just as the women of the Children of Israel were forbidden."**

​So fear Allah, O servants of Allah! When the enemies—as well as corrupt and hypocritical women—realized that this country is committed to the Abaya, and that the Abaya is a covering over one's clothes just like the Jilbab: "to draw their outer garments close over themselves." The Jilbab is the large outer cloak that covers a woman from her head to her feet, adding a layer over her clothes—a concealment over a concealment. The Abaya is just like it.

​When they realized that the women in this country would not abandon the Abaya, they altered the Abaya itself and turned it into a garment of temptation. They embroidered it, decorated it, fitted it at the waist, and thinned its fabric until it reached a point where if the woman were to abandon it, it would actually be better for her.

​So fear Allah, O servants of Allah!

You see your women and your daughters, and you hear what happens among women at parties, yet no one speaks out against it, no one shows Gheerah (protective jealousy), and no one prevents his women from going to these plagued places. So fear Allah regarding your women! Pay attention to them and do not neglect them. They go out from the beginning of the night until its end, staying up late, laughing, making merry, and engaging in matters that only Allah knows of, while their guardian knows nothing about it.

​A woman might even travel with a driver who is not a Mahram (legal male guardian) for her, and return with him, while her husband or guardian is asleep in his bed, not knowing where she went or when she will return—he knows nothing about her. If he owned sheep or cattle, would he leave them to go out and roam freely at night without knowing where they went? No, by Allah, he would not! So how can he leave his daughters, sisters, wife, and female relatives, allowing them to go out into the dark night, not knowing where they are going, whom they are going with, or whom they are sitting with?

​So fear Allah, O servants of Allah, before a wrath from Allah befalls you which you cannot withstand. Punishments are currently surrounding you on your right and your left, raging across lands in the form of killing, displacement, destruction, floods, landslides, earthquakes, and transformations.

​Fear Allah before He encompasses you all with a punishment from Himself, after which you will supplicate to Him, but He will not answer you.

​May Allah bless me and you through the Great Quran, and may He bless me and you through the verses and the Wise Remembrance it contains.

​I say these words of mine, and I seek forgiveness from Allah and repent to Him. So seek His forgiveness; indeed, He is the All-Forgiving, the Most Merciful.

## The Second Sermon

​Praise be to Allah for His bounty and favor, and I thank Him for His guidance and benevolence. I bear witness that there is no deity worthy of worship except Allah alone, without any partner; and I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and Messenger. May the peace and blessings of Allah be upon him, his family, and his companions in abundance.

​To proceed:

​O people! Fear Allah the Almighty within yourselves, and regarding your families and your children. Know that the proponents of Westernization, both from abroad and from within, desire to strip the woman of the laws of Islam, to clothe her in the attire of the West, and to divest her of all modesty and decency so that she becomes just like the disbelieving woman. This is what they desire.

​For this reason, they have spoken out against the Hijab, they have advocated for the free mixing of sexes, and they have challenged the restrictions on women's travel—which the Lawgiver (Allah) restricted and defined. They want to untether the woman from every divine law that preserves, protects, and safeguards her, so that she becomes like the Western woman, like the disbelieving woman—possessing neither morals, nor religion, nor modesty, nor chastity—living like cattle. There is no might nor power except with Allah!

​Where is your guardianship over your women? Did Allah (Glorified and Exalted be He) not say to you: "Men are the caretakers of women"? Where is your guardianship over women? Why do you abandon them to the hungry, predatory wolves? Do you blindly trust them? Do you feel entirely secure about them?

​Will you not fear Allah, O Muslims? You are living in a time of tribulations, in an era of evils. Whatever is not being done at parties and social gatherings is displayed on screens and satellite channels, which women watch and copy right inside your homes—these things are broadcasted right inside your homes! Will you not fear Allah (Glorified and Exalted be He)? Will you not protect your women and your children, and fulfill the trust that Allah placed upon you and about which He will question you on the Day of Resurrection?

​So fear Allah, O servants of Allah!

​And know that the best of speech is the Book of Allah, and the best of guidance is the guidance of the Messenger of Allah, Muhammad ﷺ. The worst of matters are those newly invented [in religion], and every innovation is a misguidance. And hold fast to the community, for indeed, the Hand of Allah is with the community, and whoever strays, strays into the Hellfire.

**​"Indeed, Allah and His angels confer blessings upon the Prophet. O you who have believed, ask [Allah to confer] blessings upon him and ask [Allah to grant him] peace."**

​O Allah, send peace and blessings upon Your servant and Messenger, our Prophet Muhammad, and be pleased, O Allah, with his Rightly Guided Caliphs, the guiding leaders: Abu Bakr, Umar, Uthman, and Ali; and with all of his companions, the Tabi'un (successors), and those who follow them in righteousness until the Day of Judgment.

​O Allah, grant honor to Islam and the Muslims, humiliate polytheism and the polytheists, destroy the enemies of the religion, and make this country safe and secure, as well as all other Muslim countries in general, O Lord of the Worlds. O Allah, grant victory to Your religion, Your Book, and the Sunnah of Your Prophet. O Allah, support those who support the religion, and forsake and destroy those who forsake the religion.

​O Allah, grant us security in our homelands, rectify our leaders, and place our governance under the care of those who fear You, have consciousness of You, and seek Your pleasure, O Lord of the Worlds. O Allah, appoint over us the best of us, shield us from the evil of the worst of us, do not place over us—due to our sins—those who neither fear You nor show mercy to us, and protect us from the evil of trials, both open and hidden. **"Our Lord, accept [this] from us. Indeed, You are the Hearing, the Knowing."**

​Servants of Allah! **"Indeed, Allah orders justice and good conduct and giving to relatives and forbids immorality and bad conduct and oppression. He admonishes you that perhaps you will be reminded."**

**​"And fulfill the covenant of Allah when you have taken it, [O believers], and do not break oaths after their confirmation while you have made Allah, over you, a witness. Indeed, Allah knows what you do."**

​So remember Allah and He will remember you; thank Him for His blessings and He will increase you [in favor]. And the remembrance of Allah is greater, and Allah knows what you do.
