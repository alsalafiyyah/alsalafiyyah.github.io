### Question: 

What is the authenticity of the claim that the conflict between Muslims and Jews is a conflict over land and borders, rather than religion and creed?

### Answer: 

We ask Allah for well-being. This is a misguided and deviant person—may Allah protect us from such a thing. It is not a dispute over borders and kingdoms; it is a dispute regarding religion. If they followed the religion of the Muslims, the lands would be one; the lands of the Muslims are one.
