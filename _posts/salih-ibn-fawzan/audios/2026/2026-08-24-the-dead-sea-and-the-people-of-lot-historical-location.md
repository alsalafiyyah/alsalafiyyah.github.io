### Question:

Is it true that the site of the punishment of the people of Lot is what is called today the Dead Sea? And if so, what is the ruling on using the mud surrounding that sea for medical treatment/therapy?

### Answer:

It is said that it is, and Allah knows best. It is called the Sea of Lot and the Dead Sea, and Allah knows best!
