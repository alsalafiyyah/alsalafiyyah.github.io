### Question:

One of my relatives says that when people were carrying the funeral bier of my grandfather, it felt very light, and my grandfather was a righteous man. Is it true that the angels help people carry the funeral bier if the man is righteous?

### Answer:

If that were the case, then carrying it wouldn't even be necessary—the angels would just take it and carry it straight to the grave! Such things should not be believed.
