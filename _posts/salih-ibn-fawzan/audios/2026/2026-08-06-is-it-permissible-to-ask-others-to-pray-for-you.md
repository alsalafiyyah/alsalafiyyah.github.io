### Question: 

May Allah be good to you, Your Eminence Shaykh, this questioner asks: Is it permissible to ask others to make supplication (dua) for one?

### Answer: 

Yes, that is fine. Asking righteous people to make supplication for you is fine. Saying, "Supplicate to Allah for me for such-and-such," is fine.
