### Question: 

May Allah reward you. Someone stated in a book that the creed of Shaykh Muhammad ibn Abd al-Wahhab aligns with the creed of the Salaf in general. Is this statement correct?

### ​Answer: 

Praise be to Allah that he acknowledged it aligns with the creed of the Salaf in general. We demand that he provide the specific details of the matters where the Shaykh allegedly did not agree. We demand this of him! It is sufficient that he admitted it aligns with the creed of the Salaf in general, so we now challenge him to produce the details regarding where he claims it disagreed with the Salaf; otherwise, he is a liar. Yes.
