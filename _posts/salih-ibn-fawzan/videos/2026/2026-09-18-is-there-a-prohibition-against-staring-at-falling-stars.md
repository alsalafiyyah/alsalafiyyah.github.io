### Question: 

Is there any prohibition from the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) against following [one's] gaze when a star shoots across or falls?

### Answer: 

No. However, this is warned against from a medical standpoint because it affects the eyesight—from a medical standpoint because it affects the eyesight.
