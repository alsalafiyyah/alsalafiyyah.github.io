### Question

Your Eminence, Shaykh, may Allah grant you success, this questioner says: Is it true that abandoning the enjoining of good and the forbidding of evil is one of the impediments to the acceptance of du'a' (supplication)?

### Answer: 

Yes, indeed. [The Prophet ﷺ said:] **"Then you will call upon Allah and He will not answer you."** It has come in the Hadith that if you abandon the enjoining of good and the forbidding of evil, you will call upon Allah and He will not answer you.
