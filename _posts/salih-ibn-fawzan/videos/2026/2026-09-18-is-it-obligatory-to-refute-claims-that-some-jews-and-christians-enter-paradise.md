### Question: 

Is it obligatory to object to someone who says in a gathering: "Indeed, some of the Jews and Christians are in Paradise"?

### Answer: 

Yes, indeed. He is to be objected to; he is to be objected to. Because this is misleading—this is misleading speech. Who are the Jews and Christians [being referred to] who are in Paradise? The terms 'Jews' and 'Christians' have become blameworthy; they have become blameworthy, so people should not be confused by this.
