### Question: 

Some people raise wild beasts (predators) and snakes in their homes, and adopt this as a profession. Is this action permissible?

### Answer: 

What is his intention behind this? If he sells predators or sells snakes, this is forbidden and not permissible. If he wants to gather snakes and predators in his house and leave them loose, the person who does this action is not of sound mind.
