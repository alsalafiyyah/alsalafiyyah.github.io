We listened to and read the address of His Royal Highness Prince Nayef bin Abdul-Aziz Al Saud—Crown Prince, Deputy Prime Minister, and Minister of Interior, may Allah protect him—delivered at the opening of the symposium titled **"The Salafi Methodology is a Sharia Approach and a National Requirement,"** held at Al-Imam Mohammad Ibn Saud Islamic University in Riyadh. It was a sincere and clear address affirming that this country of Saudi Arabia, praise be to Allah, has been and remains founded upon the methodology of the Righteous Salaf in reality, not merely in claim. This is what has guaranteed its survival and continuity, by the grace of Allah, despite the crises of purification and testing it has faced—much like those experienced by the Prophet, peace be upon him, and his companions during the battles of Uhud, Al-Ahzab (the Confederates), and Hunayn, in accordance with Allah's way among His servants to distinguish the wicked from the good, so that the believers stand firm and the hypocrites are forsaken.

​He, may Allah protect him, clarified that true Salafiyyah is the methodology that derives its rulings from the Book of Allah and the Sunnah of His Messenger, peace be upon him. In doing so, it absolves itself of all false accusations attached to it and distances itself from the practices of those who falsely claim the Salafi methodology. 

For today, those who claim to be Salafis are many, but the true test lies in who genuinely fulfills this claim with sincerity, which requires first knowing the true reality of the Salafi methodology and second adhering to it.

​And Almighty Allah said: **"And the first to embrace Islam of the Muhajirun (those who migrated from Makkah to Al-Madinah) and the Ansar (the citizens of Al-Madinah who helped and gave aid to the Muhajirun) and also those who followed them exactly (in Faith). Allah is well-pleased with them as they are well-pleased with Him. He has prepared for them Gardens under which rivers flow (Paradise), to dwell therein forever. That is the supreme success."** [At-Tawbah: 100]

​Thus, the Righteous Salaf are the Muhajirun (Emigrating Companions), the Ansar (Supporters), and those who followed them with good conduct—meaning with precision and mastery in following them, by knowing their methodology so as not to follow them out of ignorance, holding fast to it, and remaining steadfast against any obstacles or discouragements. They also disavow the innovations and newly invented matters introduced by al-Khalaf (later generations) in matters of belief and worship, just as the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, said: **"For whoever among you lives after me will see much discord. So you must adhere to my Sunnah and the Sunnah of the Rightly-Guided Caliphs who-guide-aright. Hold fast to it and bite onto it with your molar teeth. And beware of newly invented matters, for every newly invented matter is bid'ah (an innovation), and every bid'ah (innovation) is misguidance."**

​Therefore, true Salafiyyah is free from extremism and radicalism, just as it is free from laxity and moral decay. Both extremes have those who adopt them and advocate for them—one under the guise of religious devotion and adherence, and the other under the guise of facilitation and tolerance.

Then he, may Allah protect him, said: "Indeed, this blessed state has been established upon the Salafi methodology since its founding at the hands of Imam Muhammad bin Saud and his covenant with Imam Muhammad bin Abd al-Wahhab, may Allah have mercy on them both, and it continues to take pride in that to this day."

​I say: Yes, this country used to suffer from backwardness, poverty, and fragmentation in both its religious and worldly affairs. It followed the path of al-khalaf (the later generations) in creed, worship, and governance, possessing no unified entity, security, or stability. Its scholars were largely upon the methodology of al-Khalaf in belief, superstitions and acts of polytheism were widespread in worship, and chaos enveloped its governance—until Allah favored it with the call of monotheism led by the renewing Imam Muhammad bin Abd al-Wahhab, who was supported and aided in this by Imam Muhammad bin Saud. Thus, a blessed state was established upon the methodology of the Book and the Sunnah, just as Prince Nayef, may Allah protect him, stated. It remains upon this path and will continue to do so, by the grace of Allah, as long as it holds fast to that upon which it was founded.

​Then he, may Allah protect him, said: "And it realizes that whoever disparages its methodology or raises suspicions and accusations against it is ignorant and deserves to have the truth clarified to him."

And I say: Indeed, this blessed state, since its inception, has faced accusations and the raising of suspicions regarding its methodology. This is not strange, for the Prophet, peace be upon him, faced a great deal of suspicions and accusations from the Jews, Christians, and polytheists—much of which Allah mentioned in the Noble Quran, responding to it and refuting it.

​The exact same thing has been faced by this state since its establishment, and its methodology continues to be targeted by enemies, envious individuals, hypocrites, innovators, and proponents of superstitions, as attested to by its history and the books of its scholars from the era of Shaykh Muhammad bin Abd al-Wahhab to this very day. Let us read the book "Kashf al-Shubuhat" (The Clarification of Doubts) by Shaykh Muhammad bin Abd al-Wahhab, as well as the books written after it in refutation of those doubts and accusations.

Then he, may Allah protect him, said: "The hosting of this symposium by this university is nothing more than a part of that clarification and explanation of truths regarding this upright approach, which has falsely and slanderously borne more than it can endure of lies, falsehoods, and distorted concepts—such as Takfir (declaring Muslims disbelievers), extremism, terrorism, and others. This makes it incumbent upon all of us to stand united in defense of it, and to confront those suspicions and false claims with what refutes them and demonstrates their lack of truth."

​And I say: Praise be to Allah, all of these falsehoods and doubts have been refuted in numerous publications by the scholars of this Da'wah (call) as well as their brother scholars in various lands, and these works are printed and widely circulated. I suggest that Al-Imam University reprint them and distribute them as a complementary work to the project of this blessed symposium.

​Even though some opposed this call and ultimately failed—praise be to Allah—it was also supported and backed by trustworthy scholars from various regions, including "the Levant" (al-Sham), Egypt, India, and other countries. Groups calling for monotheism upon the methodology of this blessed movement also emerged—such as Jamiyat Ahl-Hadith in India, Ansar al-Sunnah in Egypt and Sudan, and Ahl al-Sunnah wal-Tawhid in Yemen. Thus, the plots of those who schemed against it ended in failure, as the Almighty said: "They want to extinguish the light of Allah with their mouths, but Allah refuses except to perfect His light, although the disbelievers dislike it." [At-Tawbah: 32]

Then he, may Allah protect him, said: "My dear brothers, we assure you that this state will remain—by the permission of Allah—adherent to the upright Salafi methodology, and it will neither deviate from it nor compromise on it. It is the source of its glory, success, and elevation, just as it is the source of its advancement and progress, because it combines authenticity with modernity. It is both a religious, legal methodology and a worldly methodology that calls for adopting the means of progress and advancement, calling for peaceful coexistence with others and respecting their rights."

​And I say: In this, there is a promise from His Highness to the people of the Sunnah in this country and elsewhere regarding the steadfastness of the Saudi state—may Allah grant it success—upon the methodology of the Righteous Salaf upon which it was established. It also brings despair to the enemies of this country who attempt to dislodge it from its upright methodology toward deviant methodologies adopted by those who wish to wrest power (like partisan groups), those who wish to eradicate the Sunnah and replace it with innovation, Sufism, and superstitions in this land (like Sufi and superstitious groups), or those who wish to shift it away from moderation toward either extremism and radicalism (like the ignorant and extremist groups) or laxity and dilution (like the Murji'ah, secularists, and liberals).

He, may Allah protect him, said: "Because this methodology which it adheres to is the source of its glory, success, and elevation."

​And I say: Witness to this is the state of chaos, loss, dispersion, deprivation, and fragmentation that this country experienced before this blessed state.

​And regarding his statement, may Allah protect him, that this methodology combines authenticity and modernity, I say: This is because this religion is suitable for every time and place, as Imam Malik, may Allah have mercy on him, said: "Nothing will rectify the latter part of this ummah except that which rectified its first part."

He, may Allah protect him, said: "It is both a religious, legal methodology and a worldly methodology that calls for adopting the means of progress and advancement."

And I say: The proof of this is found in the Almighty's saying: **"This day, I have perfected your religion for you..."** [Al-Ma'idah: 3], 

His saying: **"And make ready against them all you can of power..."** [Al-Anfal: 60]

His saying: **"O you who believe! Take your precautions..."** [An-Nisa: 71]

And His saying: **"...and do not throw yourselves into destruction (by not spending your wealth in the Cause of Allah)..."** [Al-Baqarah: 195].

​And his statement, may Allah protect him: "And calling for peaceful coexistence with others and respecting their rights."

​And I say: This is what is embodied in the Almighty's saying: **"...but transgress not the limits. Truly, Allah likes not the transgressors. [This Verse is the first one that was revealed in connection with Jihad, but it was supplemented by another (V. 9:36)]."** [Al-Baqarah: 190], 

His saying: **"So long, as they are true to you, stand you true to them."** [At-Tawbah: 7], 

His saying: **"...and let not the enmity and hatred of others make you avoid justice. Be just: that is nearer to piety..."** [Al-Ma'idah: 8], 

And His saying: **"Allah does not forbid you to deal justly and kindly with those who fought not against you on account of religion and did not drive you out of your homes. Verily, Allah loves those who deal with equity."** [Al-Mumtahanah: 8].

​May Allah bless His Highness, place what he said in the scale of his good deeds, and may Allah bestow prayers and peace upon our Prophet Muhammad.
