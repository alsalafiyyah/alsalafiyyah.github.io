Shaykh al-Islam ibn Taymiyyah, may Allah Ta'ala have mercy on him said:

Whoever turns away from the divinely prescribed path of the Salaf must inevitably fall into misguidance and contradiction, remaining trapped in either compound ignorance or simple ignorance.
