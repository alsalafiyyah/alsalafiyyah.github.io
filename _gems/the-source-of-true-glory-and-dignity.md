King Saud bin Abdulaziz Al Saud—may Allah Almighty have mercy on him—said: 

Ever since we assumed the reins of power, our primary concern has been to hold fast to the Book of Allah (the Qur'an), be guided by the guidance of the Messenger of Allah, and follow the traditions of his successors among the righteous Salaf (al-Salaf al-Salih).

He—may Allah Almighty have mercy on him—also said: 

The greatest of these principles is adherence to the Book of Allah, the Sunnah of His Prophet, and believing what the righteous Salaf believed—those whose past you know, and who attained glory and dignity.
