Imam Abdul-Aziz bin Muhammad bin Saud (Abdul-Aziz I)—may Allah Ta'ala have mercy on him—said: 

We are followers of the Qur'an, the Sunnah, the righteous Salaf (predecessors) of the Ummah (Muslim nation), and what is relied upon from the statements of the four Imams.
