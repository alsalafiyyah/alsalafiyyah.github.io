Prince Sultan bin Abdul-Aziz—may Allah Almighty have mercy on him—said in a speech delivered before the leaders of the Armed Forces: 

"We are envied and fought against for the blessing that Allah has given us, and every possessor of a blessing is envied."

Prince Sultan explained that his country is currently facing a war regarding its religion, being accused regarding what it considers an honor for itself, which is "Al-Salafiyyah." 

Defending it, he said: "The righteous predecessors (al-Salaf al-Salih) are the role model; if we are Salafis, we are following the righteous predecessors."
