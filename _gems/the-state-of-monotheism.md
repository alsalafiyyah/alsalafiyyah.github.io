King Fahd bin Abdulaziz Al Saud—may Allah Almighty have mercy on him—said:  

The unifier of this peninsula and the gatherer of its unity, King Abdulaziz—may Allah have mercy on him—came to establish the state of monotheism, Islamic Sharia, and the Salafi da'wah—the symbol of true Islam and its scholarly manifestation, where religion and worldly life are intertwined, and the entire nation walks upon the path of Allah, the path of sincere jihad and persevering labor.
