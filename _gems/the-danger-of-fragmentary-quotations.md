Shaykh Salih ibn Fawzan al-Fawzan (may Allah Ta'ala preserve him) said: 

If it is necessary to quote the statements of the scholars, one must quote them fully from beginning to end, gather the scholar's statements on the issue from his various books so that his intent becomes clear, refer some of his statements back to others, and not suffice with quoting just one part while leaving out the other. For this causes misunderstanding and attributes to the scholar what he never intended.
