Ibn Hazm, may Allah have mercy on him, said: 

As for what they [the Christians] say regarding the claim of the Rawafid (Rafidah Shia) that the Qur'an has been altered: the Rawafid are not Muslims; rather, they are a sect whose origins arose twenty-five years after the death of the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him. They are a faction that follows the path of the Jews and Christians in falsehood and disbelief.
