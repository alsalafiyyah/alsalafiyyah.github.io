### Lineage

He is the Eminent Sheikh and Scholar: **Abdul-Aziz bin Abdullah bin Abdul-Rahman Al-Rajhi**. His lineage traces back to the renowned **Bani Zayd** tribe in Najd, which descends from **Quda'ah**—a famous Qahtanite tribe.

### Birth

He—may Allah preserve him—was born in the city of **Al-Bukayriyah** in the Al-Qassim region in **1361 AH** (approx. 1942 CE).

---

### Seeking Knowledge and His Teachers

The Sheikh completed his primary education in Al-Bukayriyah, then joined the **Scientific Institute in Riyadh**, followed by the **Faculty of Sharia**. He subsequently earned a Master's degree from the **High Institute of Judiciary** at the same university, with a thesis titled: *Taqlid, Ifta, and Istifta* (Imitation, Issuing Rulings, and Seeking Rulings).

* **Early Recitation:** In Al-Bukayriyah, he studied under the local Quran reciter, **Sheikh Abdul-Rahman bin Salim** (may Allah have mercy on him).
* **Early Scholars:** He attended several lessons and Friday sermons of **Sheikh Muhammad bin Ibrahim** (may Allah have mercy on him), the former Grand Mufti of Saudi Arabia (d. 1389 AH).

**He studied under a number of prominent scholars, including:**

* **Sheikh Abdullah bin Humaid** (d. 1402 AH), former Chairman of the Supreme Judicial Council, under whom he studied *Al-Kafi* by Ibn Qudamah Al-Maqdisi.
* **Sheikh Abdul-Aziz bin Abdullah bin Baz** (d. 1419 AH), former Grand Mufti of Saudi Arabia. Sheikh Ibn Baz was his primary mentor whom he accompanied closely in study circles for a long period until his death. He studied numerous texts under him, including the complete *Sunan An-Nasa'i*, parts of *Sahih Al-Bukhari*, *Muntaqa Al-Akhbar*, *Kitab At-Tawheed* by Ibn Khuzaymah, *Al-Rawd Al-Murbi'*, and others.
* **Sheikh Abdul-Rahman bin Nasir Al-Barrak** (may Allah preserve him).
* **Sheikh Abdul-Aziz bin Abdullah bin Subayyil** (d. 1412 AH), judge of Al-Bukayriyah, Al-Khabra, and Al-Badaya, under whom he studied while in Al-Bukayriyah.

**At the university, he studied under leading scholars, such as:**

* **Sheikh Abdul-Razzaq Afifi** (may Allah have mercy on him).
* **Sheikh Abdullah bin Ghudayyan** (may Allah have mercy on him).

The Sheikh also holds a number of scholarly certifications (*ijazat*) in the books of Hadith (Sunnah) and other Islamic disciplines.

### Roles and Contributions

* Taught at **Imam Muhammad ibn Saud Islamic University** in the Faculty of Theology (*Usul al-Din*), Department of Creed (*Aqeedah*), continuing even after his formal retirement.
* Supervised and examined dozens of Master's theses and Doctoral dissertations.
* Participated with the Ministry of Islamic Affairs, Dawah, and Guidance during Hajj seasons through lecturing and issuing rulings (*ifta*).
* Taught at the **Two Holy Mosques** (in Mecca and Medina) during various seasonal periods.
* Served as a Friday preacher (*Khateeb*) in the city of Riyadh.
* Delivers regular scientific lessons in Riyadh; his weekly schedule includes **more than twenty lessons** across various Islamic sciences.
* Conducts foundational scholarly courses in creed (*aqeedah*) and other subjects throughout Saudi Arabia and neighboring Gulf states.

---

### Scholarly Works and Publications

The Sheikh has authored approximately **seventy books** (up to the writing of this biography), including:

1. ***Minhat al-Malik al-Jalil bi-Sharh Sahih Muhammad bin Isma'il (Al-Bukhari)*** – 14 volumes.
2. ***Tawfiq al-Rabb al-Mun'im bi-Sharh Sahih al-Imam Muslim*** – 9 volumes.
3. Commentaries on a large number of treatises by Sheikh al-Islam Ibn Taymiyyah.
4. Commentaries on the treatises of Imam Muhammad bin Abdul-Wahhab.
5. ***Hall al-Uqdah bi-Sharh al-Umdah (Umdat al-Fiqh)*** – 2 volumes.
6. ***Al-Ifham bi-Sharh Bulugh al-Maram*** – 2 volumes.
7. ***Al-I'anah 'ala Taqrib al-Sharh wa al-Ibanah*** – 2 volumes.
8. ***Al-Hidayah al-Rabbaniyyah bi-Sharh al-Aqeedah al-Tahawiyyah***.
9. ***Taqlid, Ifta, and Istifta*** – His Master's thesis.

In addition, he has issued **over twenty thousand fatwas**, spanning written verdicts, open Q&A sessions, phone inquiries, and daily lessons.
