**His Background:**
He is Sheikh Abdul Razzaq bin Abdul Muhsin al-Abbad al-Badr, the son of the eminent scholar and Muhaddith of Madinah, Sheikh Abdul Muhsin al-Abbad al-Badr.

* **Birth:** Born on Jumada al-Thani 22, 1382 AH in Zulfi, Saudi Arabia.
* **Residence:** Madinah, Saudi Arabia.
* **Current Position:** Faculty member at the Islamic University of Madinah.
* **Academic Qualification:** Ph.D. in Islamic Creed (*Aqidah*).

### Notable Works and Research

Sheikh al-Badr has authored numerous books, research papers, and explanations across various Islamic disciplines, including:

1. *Fiqh al-Ad'iyah wal-Adhkar* (Understanding Supplications and Remembrances)
2. *Al-Hajj wa Tahdhib al-Nufus* (Hajj and the Purification of Souls)
3. *Tadhkirat al-Mu'tasi Sharh Aqidat Abd al-Ghani al-Maqdisi*
4. *Sharh Hashiyat Abi Dawud*
5. *Dirasah li-Athar Malik fi al-Istiwa'*

### Audio Lectures and Explanations

He has recorded comprehensive audio explanations for many classic texts, such as:

* *Sharh al-Qawa'id al-Muthla*
* *Sharh al-Kalim al-Tayyib*
* *Sharh Qawa'id al-Asma' al-Husna li-Ibn al-Qayyim*
* *Sharh al-Aqidah al-Wasitiyyah* (delivered at the Grand Mosque)
* *Sharh al-Aqidah al-Tahawiyyah*
* A complete commentary on *Al-Adab al-Mufrad* by Imam al-Bukhari
* *Sharh Kitab al-Fusul fi Sirat al-Rasul* by Ibn Kathir, and many others available on his [Official Website](http://www.al-badr.net/).

### Prominent Teachers

He studied under and benefited from several senior scholars, most notably:

1. His father, the eminent scholar Sheikh **Abdul Muhsin al-Abbad** (may Allah preserve him)
2. The grand scholar Sheikh **Abdul Aziz bin Baz** (may Allah have mercy on him)
3. The grand scholar Sheikh **Muhammad al-Salih al-Uthaymeen** (may Allah have mercy on him)
4. Sheikh **Muhammad Nasir al-Din al-Albani** (may Allah have mercy on him)
5. Sheikh **Ali Nasir Faqeehi** (may Allah preserve him)
