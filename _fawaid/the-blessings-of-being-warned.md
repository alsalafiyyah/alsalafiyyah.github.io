scholar: "Shaykh Ibn Baz"

summary: "A sound-minded person should not become angry when they are warned against falsehood or when they are alerted to bidahs (innovations in religion)."
---

A sound-minded person should not become angry when they are warned against falsehood or when they are alerted to bidahs (innovations in religion). Instead, one should say: **'Praise be to Allah who guided me, praise be to Allah who made me realize that this is an innovation, and praise be to Allah who directed me toward goodness.'** 

It is incumbent upon a person to learn, gain insight, and not blindly follow people. The Prophet ﷺ said in an authentic hadith: **"Whomever Allah intends good for, He grants him understanding of the religion."** and he ﷺ said: **"Whoever treads a path seeking knowledge, Allah will make easy for him a path to Paradise."**
